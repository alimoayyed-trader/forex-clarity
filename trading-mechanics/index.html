01 · ORDER TYPES

How Do Forex Orders Work?

A trading order is an instruction to a broker or trading platform to open or close a position under specified conditions.

Common order types include:

  • Market Order: An instruction to trade at the best currently available price.
  • Limit Order: An instruction to trade at a specified price or a better one.
  • Stop Order: An instruction that becomes active when the market reaches a specified price.

02 · LOT SIZES

What Is a Lot in Forex?

A lot represents the size or volume of a forex position. Position size affects how much money is gained or lost when the exchange rate changes.

  • Standard Lot: 100,000 units.
  • Mini Lot: 10,000 units.
  • Micro Lot: 1,000 units.
  • Nano Lot: 100 units, where supported.

Actual contract specifications can vary between brokers and instruments.

03 · LEVERAGE

How Does Leverage Work?

Leverage allows a trader to control a market position larger than the capital allocated to that position.

For example, leverage of 1:100 means that 1,000 units of account currency may provide exposure equivalent to 100,000 units, subject to the instrument and broker requirements.

Leverage increases both potential gains and potential losses. It does not reduce the underlying market risk.

04 · MARGIN

What Is Margin in Forex?

Margin is the amount of money required by a broker to open and maintain a leveraged trading position.

It is not the same as a trading fee. It is capital set aside as collateral while a position is open.

Traders should understand used margin, free margin and margin level because these figures affect their ability to maintain open positions.

05 · POSITION EXITS

Stop-Loss and Take-Profit

A stop-loss order is designed to close a position when the market reaches a specified adverse price.

A take-profit order is designed to close a position when the market reaches a specified favorable price.

These orders can help structure trade exits, but execution prices are not guaranteed in every market condition. Gaps and slippage may affect the final result.

06 · TRADE EXECUTION

How Are Forex Trades Executed?

Trade execution is the process through which a trading instruction is processed and a position is opened, modified or closed.

Execution can be affected by available liquidity, market volatility, spreads, broker infrastructure and the type of order used.

The requested price and the final execution price may differ, particularly during fast-moving markets.

07 · TRADING COSTS

What Does It Cost to Trade Forex?

Forex trading can involve several costs that affect the overall result of a position.

  • Spread: The difference between the bid and ask prices.
  • Commission: A fee charged by some brokers for executing trades.
  • Swap or Financing: A charge or credit that may apply when positions are held overnight.
  • Slippage: The difference between the expected price and the actual execution price.

IMPORTANT

Understand the mechanics before risking capital.

Leverage, margin and position size can significantly affect trading risk. Understanding how orders work does not guarantee profitable trading.

Learn the mechanics first, practice with a demo account where appropriate, and understand the risks before considering real-money trading.
