FOREX BASICS

What Is Forex? A Simple Guide for Beginners

Learn what forex means, how currency pairs work, how traders buy and sell currencies, and the basic concepts you should understand before trading.

BEGINNER GUIDE

If you have ever exchanged money before traveling to another country, you have already interacted with the basic idea behind the forex market. You give one currency and receive another. The amount you receive depends on the exchange rate between those two currencies.

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global market where currencies are bought and sold. Banks, businesses, governments, financial institutions and individual traders all participate in the foreign exchange market for different reasons.

You do not need to understand the entire financial system to understand forex. This guide starts with the basics and gradually explains currency pairs, pips, spreads, lots, leverage, brokers and the risks involved in forex trading.

What Is Forex?

Forex, or foreign exchange, is the market for exchanging one currency for another.

Unlike a stock, where you generally buy or sell shares of a company, forex trading involves a relationship between two currencies.

For example, when you see EUR/USD at 1.1000, you are looking at the value of the euro expressed in US dollars. In simple terms, the quote means that one euro is valued at 1.10 US dollars at that price.

Forex is largely an over-the-counter (OTC) market rather than one single physical exchange. Trading takes place through a global network of banks, financial institutions, companies and other market participants.

How Does Forex Work?

Forex is traded through currency pairs. A currency pair compares the value of one currency with another.

The first currency is called the base currency and the second currency is called the quote currency.

Take EUR/USD as an example:

  • EUR = base currency
  • USD = quote currency
  • EUR/USD = 1.1000 means one euro is quoted at 1.10 US dollars

If EUR/USD rises from 1.1000 to 1.1100, the euro has increased in value relative to the US dollar based on that exchange rate.

If EUR/USD falls from 1.1000 to 1.0900, the euro has decreased in value relative to the dollar.

This relationship is the foundation of forex trading. You are not simply asking whether one currency will rise by itself. You are considering whether one currency will become stronger or weaker relative to another currency.

What Is a Currency Pair?

A currency pair contains two currencies. The first is the base currency and the second is the quote currency.

Base Currency

The base currency is the first currency shown in a pair. In EUR/USD, the euro is the base currency.

Quote Currency

The quote currency is the second currency in the pair. It tells you how much of that currency is needed to buy one unit of the base currency.

Example: EUR/USD

If EUR/USD is 1.1000, one euro is quoted as being worth 1.10 US dollars.

What Are Major, Minor, and Exotic Currency Pairs?

Currency pairs are often grouped into three broad categories: major pairs, minor pairs and exotic pairs.

Major Currency Pairs

Major pairs involve the US dollar and another major currency. Examples include EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY and USD/CHF.

Minor Currency Pairs

Minor pairs, sometimes called cross pairs, generally involve major currencies without the US dollar. Examples include EUR/GBP, EUR/JPY and GBP/JPY.

Exotic Currency Pairs

Exotic pairs generally combine a major currency with a currency from a smaller or emerging economy. Trading conditions can differ significantly between currency pairs, including liquidity and spreads.

How Do Traders Make Money in Forex?

Forex traders generally try to benefit from changes in exchange rates.

If a trader expects a currency pair to rise, they may open a buy position. If they expect the pair to fall, they may open a sell position.

Buying a Currency Pair

Buying EUR/USD means taking a position that benefits if the euro strengthens relative to the US dollar, assuming other costs and conditions remain unchanged.

Selling a Currency Pair

Selling EUR/USD means taking a position that benefits if the euro weakens relative to the US dollar, again assuming other costs and conditions remain unchanged.

Long and Short Positions

A long position generally means buying with the expectation that price will rise. A short position generally means selling with the expectation that price will fall.

What Are Pips, Spreads, and Lots?

Three terms beginners will encounter frequently are pips, spreads and lots.

What Is a Pip?

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring a small change in a forex exchange rate.

For many commonly traded currency pairs, a pip is associated with the fourth decimal place. Some currency pairs, such as many yen pairs, use a different convention.

What Is the Spread?

The spread is the difference between the bid price and the ask price. It is one of the basic trading costs a trader should understand before opening a position.

What Is a Lot?

A lot is a standard way of describing the size of a forex position. Different lot sizes represent different amounts of currency exposure, so position size directly affects how much a price movement can gain or lose.

What Is Leverage in Forex?

Leverage allows a trader to control a larger market position with a smaller amount of capital.

This can make relatively small price movements have a larger effect on the trader's account.

Leverage therefore increases exposure. It can magnify potential gains, but it can also magnify losses.

What Moves Forex Prices?

Currency prices can move for many different reasons. There is no single factor that controls the forex market.

Some important influences include:

  • Interest rates and central bank decisions
  • Inflation and economic growth
  • Employment and economic data
  • Political and geopolitical developments
  • Market expectations and investor sentiment
  • Supply and demand for currencies

Traders often combine economic information with price analysis to form a view of the market.

Who Participates in the Forex Market?

The forex market includes participants with very different objectives.

  • Central banks
  • Commercial and investment banks
  • International companies
  • Investment firms and other financial institutions
  • Forex brokers
  • Individual traders

A company may exchange currencies to make an international payment, while a trader may take a position because they expect an exchange rate to change.

When Is the Forex Market Open?

The global forex market operates across major financial centers and is generally available around the clock during the business week.

Trading activity changes throughout the day as different financial centers become active. For example, the London and New York sessions are closely watched by many traders because of their market activity and overlap.

What Is a Forex Broker?

A forex broker is a company or financial service provider that gives traders access to trading services and markets.

Brokers can differ in areas such as regulation, trading conditions, spreads, commissions, execution, available instruments and platform options.

Before using a broker, traders should understand the applicable regulations, fees, trading conditions and risks.

What Are the Risks of Forex Trading?

Forex trading involves financial risk. Price movements can happen quickly, and leverage can increase the size of both potential gains and potential losses.

Trading costs, spreads, commissions, execution conditions and position size can also affect the outcome of a trade.

Learning position sizing, stop-loss planning and risk management is therefore an important part of learning forex.

Is Forex Suitable for Beginners?

Beginners can learn how the forex market works, but understanding the concepts is different from being ready to trade real money.

A sensible learning process starts with the basics: currency pairs, price movements, trading orders, position size, leverage and risk management.

Practice and careful study can help a learner understand how the market behaves before making decisions involving real capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is forex trading?

Forex trading involves buying or selling currency pairs with the goal of benefiting from changes in exchange rates, while accepting the financial risks involved.

How does the forex market work?

Forex compares the value of one currency with another through currency pairs. Traders can buy or sell those pairs based on their expectations about future price movements.

Can you make money trading forex?

It is possible to make profits from forex trading, but losses are also possible. Results depend on many factors, including market conditions, trading decisions, position size, costs and risk management.

Is forex trading risky?

Yes. Forex trading involves financial risk, and leverage can increase exposure to both gains and losses.

Is forex suitable for beginners?

Beginners can learn forex, but learning the market and managing risk should come before trading real money.
