FOREX BASICS

What Is Forex?

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global market where one currency is exchanged for another. This guide explains how forex works and the basic concepts beginners should understand.

BEGINNER GUIDE

What Is Forex?

Forex, or foreign exchange, refers to the buying and selling of currencies. When currencies are exchanged, the value of one currency is expressed in relation to another.

How Does Forex Work?

Forex is traded in currency pairs. Instead of buying or selling a single currency by itself, a forex transaction compares the value of one currency with another.

For example, EUR/USD represents the euro against the US dollar. If EUR/USD is quoted at 1.1000, the price expresses the value of one euro in US dollars.

What Is a Currency Pair?

A currency pair contains two currencies. The first currency is called the base currency, while the second is called the quote currency.

Base Currency

The base currency is the first currency shown in a currency pair.

Quote Currency

The quote currency is the second currency. It is used to express the value of the base currency.

Example: EUR/USD

In EUR/USD, EUR is the base currency and USD is the quote currency. A price of 1.1000 means that one euro is valued at 1.10 US dollars.

What Are Pips?

A pip is a commonly used unit for measuring a small change in a forex exchange rate. Traders often use pips when discussing price movement, potential profit or loss, and trading risk.

What Is the Spread?

The spread is the difference between the bid price and the ask price. It is one of the basic trading costs that traders should understand before trading.

What Is a Forex Broker?

A forex broker is a company that provides traders with access to trading services and financial markets. Brokers can differ in areas such as trading conditions, fees, execution, available instruments and regulation.

Who Participates in the Forex Market?

The forex market includes different types of participants. These can include commercial and central banks, financial institutions, corporations, investment firms, brokers and individual traders.

What Are the Risks of Forex Trading?

Forex trading involves financial risk. Leverage can increase market exposure and can therefore increase both potential gains and potential losses.

Understanding risk management, position sizing and trade execution is important before using real money.

What Should You Learn Next?

If you are new to forex, the next step is to understand trading mechanics, currency pairs, charts, market structure and basic risk management.
